Privacy information

Effective October 17, 2026. Developer: Larry Kuzniak. Contact: larrykuzniak@mac.com.

Bundled iPad edition

The included iPad source is designed to run its game files on your device. It includes no advertising, tracking, analytics, remote gameplay server, or account requirement. Game progress and custom scenarios are saved in app-local storage, with a local settings backup for continuity. The developer does not receive your progress automatically.

Files you choose

You may export saves or scenarios to a destination you select, or import files you select in Files. Exported files contain game state and scenario data. Their storage and sharing depend on your chosen destination. Deleting the app removes its local data, subject to device backup behavior. Keep exported backups for anything you wish to retain.

Voluntary support

If you email the developer, your email address, message, and attachments are received for answering the request and investigating feedback. Do not send sensitive personal information. Contact the developer to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to applicable retention obligations. Email delivery uses your email provider and the developer's provider.

Hosted web edition

The web game saves progress in browser-local storage and caches game files for offline play. Its current host may require ChatGPT sign-in and process account information and operational request logs under that host's policies. Those hosting practices are separate from the bundled offline app. Clearing website data removes local web progress and caches.

Changes

If data practices change, this page and the App Store privacy answers must be updated. The final signed app's privacy disclosures must be verified before release.
