Support & credits

Utah Command is offered free. There are no advertisements, subscriptions, or in-app purchases in this edition. Larry intends to keep it free and does not intend to sell it.

Bug reports and suggestions

Email larrykuzniak@mac.com. Include game edition, scenario, turn, rules enabled, device and iPadOS version, reproduction steps, expected result, and actual result. Attach a screenshot or exported save if useful. Please omit private information. Feedback is welcome; response times and fixes are not guaranteed.
