Getting started

Utah Command - Command edition 0.19 / distribution package 1.0-preparation
Developed and designed by Larry Kuzniak. A free, single-player, turn-based strategy game.

Start on Windows

Extract the full Windows ZIP, then double-click Start-Utah-Command.cmd. Keep its launcher window open while playing. Bundled files work offline. Use the same browser profile for local saves. Read READ-ME-FIRST.txt for details.

Your first battle

  1. Use Scenarios to choose Utah · Beach Exits. Read its briefing, objective flags, turn limit, available supply, and air missions.
  2. Leave Advanced Rules and Operational depth off for your first battle. Leave automatic supply priorities in use. Select First game for the guided tour.
  3. Tap a blue American formation or its name in the roster. A gold halo and SELECTED label identify it. Gray counters are German; red counters represent British or Canadian troops where present.
  4. Tap Move, then a legal highlighted destination. Or tap Attack and a spotted enemy. Infantry and tanks attack adjacent hexes; artillery has a four-hex range. Issue orders to other formations.
  5. Tap Resolve turn to execute orders and let the computer respond. Read the situation report. Repeat until the scenario ends.

Command by intent

Open Command missions and enable Command mode. Assign Seize, Defend, Support, or Reserve, then choose a risk policy. Missions persist across turns. A direct move or attack overrides the mission for one turn; Rest / cancel pauses it. Attached artillery and engineers work under the formation's existing authority, subject to resources and rules. Additional support may ask for approval. Air requires approval and a working Army link.

Map and supplies

Full-screen map makes more room; Menu returns to other controls. Terrain overlay shows exact hex terrain; painted background roads are illustrative. Fog of war hides unobserved forces. A question-mark contact is an old sighting. Supply Command shows delivery requests, priorities, routes, and forecasts. Priority cannot repair a blocked route.

Keep your progress

Orders and turns autosave locally. Export save makes a JSON backup; import restores a compatible backup after confirmation. Native iPad exports use the share sheet: choose Save to Files. Native imports use the Files picker. Use separate scenario export/import for custom designs. Export before deleting the app, clearing website data, changing devices or addresses, or restarting a battle. Saves do not automatically transfer between Safari, the Home Screen app, and the native app. Exit without saving rolls back this session's changes, including its autosaves.

Give someone a Windows copy

Send the complete Windows ZIP. The recipient extracts all files and runs Start-Utah-Command.cmd on a personal Windows laptop with PowerShell and a modern browser. No hosted website or ChatGPT account is required to play this edition.
